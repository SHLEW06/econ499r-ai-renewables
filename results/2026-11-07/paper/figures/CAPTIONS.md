# Figures with captions

**`fig_map_renew_share_2025.png`.** Renewable share of generation by state, 2025. Utility-scale only (EIA).

**`fig_map_ai_2025.png`.** Private data-center jobs per 1,000 workers, lagged, 2025 (BLS QCEW). AK, ND, SD and WY are suppressed only in 2025 itself, so this lag uses 2024.

**`fig_georgia_trends.png`.** Georgia and the U.S.: private data-center jobs per 1,000 workers and renewable share.

**`fig_h1_coefficients.png`.** H1: AI(t-1) per one within-state SD of AI (0.5212: winsorized lagged AI after removing state and year effects), with 95% CIs. One panel per unit. Two-way FE, ctrl4, clustered by state.

**`fig_spec_curve.png`.** The AI coefficient across specifications. Next-year AI is significant for renew_share, while AI(t-1) isn't on the same sample.

**`fig_spillover_placebo.png`.** The renew_share spillover under the timing test. It isn't significant on 2015-2023, and with border weights neighbors' future AI carries the association.

**`fig_fang_bridge.png`.** The AI coefficient from Fang's spec to ours. Data-center jobs are the more reliable extension measure.

**`fig_moderation.png`.** AI slope at the 25th and 75th percentiles of broadband and R&D share.

**`fig_georgia_fan.png`.** Georgia solar: learning-curve arithmetic to 2035 on IEA WEO 2025 paths (Annex A, Tables A.3a, A.3b and A.3c; pp. 417, 423 and 429). Not a forecast of ours.

**`fig_fang_table2_by_basis.png`.** Fang Table 2, column 6, AI coefficient by SEDS basis, with 95% CIs. June 2023 = SEDS as published before EIA's accounting change.

**`fig_fang_event_study.png`.** Event study on 2019 postings exposure, base 2021. Pre-2021 coefficients rise toward zero (jointly significant on current SEDS), so post-2021 gains look like a continuing trend.

**`fig_ext_trend_placebo.png`.** Trend placebo: ln employment in distilleries and pet care in place of AI, Fang's spec and ours.

**`fig_ext_georgia_cross_state.png`.** Across states, 2015-2025: change in data-center jobs per 1,000 against the change in solar and renewable shares; Georgia in red. Descriptive.

**`fig_ext_drivers.png`.** Year effects (two-way model, base 2015) and the path fitted from measured drivers. Correlation is consistent with the drivers' role, not proof of it.

**`fig_ext_state_slopes.png`.** Per-state slopes of commercial sales per person on AI(t-1); VA, TX and GA in red. The pooled slope is about zero.
